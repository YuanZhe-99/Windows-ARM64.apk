Storage

App data%LOCALAPPDATA%\Windows-ARM64.apk\AppsEach app keeps what it saves in a folder of its own here. 46 MB in all.
Converted app codeMade the first time each app opens, so later starts are quicker. 182 MB. Anything cleared is made again when needed.
